Density mode and error states
Two things the brief names but never specs. Density mode is for the second audience: camp coordinators working a list of forty people at speed. Error states are for the moments the app has to tell the truth without sounding like it broke.
Density mode never touches the accessibility floor. Targets stay 56dp, body stays 18sp on anything that carries meaning. What shrinks is padding, decoration and the space between rows — not the text and not the hit areas.

S · ERROR STATES · THE REST OF THEM
Rules these follow: amber, never red. Say what happened, then what the person can do, then what the app is doing meanwhile. Never blame the network, because there is no network. Never use the word failed where waited would be truthful.
