M · WHAT THE PROOF SHOWED, AND THE STRINGS TO CHECK
Findings
  • Bengali runs 15 to 30 percent longer than English at the same point size. Every one line label in the app has to survive two lines.
  • Line height 1.5 clears Bengali matras and the Devanagari shirorekha. At 1.35 the row above starts to collide.
  • Nav labels are the tightest spot. Five destinations at 12sp in Bengali need 60dp of width, which is what the 72dp slot gives.
  • At 200 percent text the emergency tiles stay two across by dropping the word to two lines, never by shrinking the pictogram.
Rules this locks in
  • No fixed height on anything containing text. Min height only.
  • No text truncation on emergency or delivery state. Wrap instead.
  • Pictogram and shape sizes never scale with the font. They are the fallback when the words get long.
  • Numerals follow the language: Bengali digits in Bengali, Devanagari digits in Hindi, and the same digits inside timestamps.
